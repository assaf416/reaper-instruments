# Demo Song

A 24-bar song (A minor, 100 BPM, **Am - F - C - G**, about 58 seconds) that plays **every instrument in this repository at once (including the Retro Synth, on two tracks)**,
so you can test them together. Open `DemoSong.rpp` in REAPER and press Play. It is generated by
[`Build-DemoSong.ps1`](Build-DemoSong.ps1) - edit the arrangement there and run it again to rewrite the project.

Run `..\Install-All.ps1` first so every plugin is installed. Loading takes a few seconds: the Drums, two Guitar instances,
the Piano and the Mellotron each read their samples when the project opens.

## Tracks

| # | Track | Plugin chain | What it plays |
|---|-------|--------------|---------------|
| 1 | Drums | Drums (Captain's Kit) | kick / snare / hats / toms / crash, ghost notes, fills |
| 2 | Bass | Guitar sampler (low Strat notes, no effects) -> **Bass Rig** (Bass Station S2) | driving 8th-note bass line |
| 3 | Guitar | Guitar sampler (Strat, light chorus, synced delay) -> **Guitar Rig** (Guitar Station S2) | palm-muted 8ths, then open power chords |
| 4 | Organ | Organ (single manual, preset 3 "Gospel") | whole-bar chords |
| 5 | Piano | Piano (Matt's Fender Rhodes) | arpeggio, backbeat stabs, 8th-note comping |
| 6 | Mellotron | Mellotron (flute + M300A violins + string section) | chord pad and a melody |
| 7 | Synth arp | Retro Synth, preset 27 "Pluck Arp" | 16th-note arpeggio through the verse and chorus |
| 8 | Synth lead | Retro Synth, preset 11 "Moore Lead" | doubles the melody an octave up in the second half of the chorus; the final note |

## Arrangement

| Bars | Section | Who plays |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 1-4 | **Intro** | Rhodes arpeggio, organ and Mellotron pad; the Mellotron melody enters in bar 3 |
| 5-12 | **Verse** | drums (light), bass, palm-muted guitar, Rhodes backbeat, organ, soft Mellotron pad, soft synth arp; a snare roll in bar 12 |
| 13-20 | **Chorus** | everything: crash on bar 13, open power chords, 8th-note Rhodes, Mellotron melody, louder synth arp, open hats; the synth lead joins in bar 17; a tom fill in bar 20 |
| 21-24 | **Outro** | drums thin out and fade, sustained chords, one last crash and chord in bar 24 |

Markers (Intro / Verse / Chorus / Outro) are in the ruler. Velocities change across the song on purpose, so you hear the velocity layers
of the drums, Rhodes and guitar (soft verse, hard chorus).

## What to listen for (test checklist)

- [ ] All six tracks make sound; the meters on every track move.
- [ ] **Drums:** the snare roll in bar 12 gets louder note by note (velocity layers); the hi-hat chokes the open hat; toms fall in pitch in bar 20.
- [ ] **Bass rig / Guitar rig:** the bass track sounds like a bass amp, the guitar track has amp and cabinet tone; bypassing the rig changes the sound.
- [ ] **Guitar delay and chorus:** the delay repeats in time (1/8, ping-pong) and the chorus widens the guitar.
- [ ] **Organ:** a warm, slowly rotating (Leslie) chord pad; try the preset buttons while it plays.
- [ ] **Rhodes:** soft arpeggio in the intro, harder comping in the chorus (different velocity layers).
- [ ] **Mellotron:** three tapes blending as a pad, the melody on top; note the tape runs out after about 8 seconds on very long notes.
- [ ] **Retro Synth:** two different presets on two tracks - a plucky arpeggio, then a singing mono lead (note the glide between lead notes).
- [ ] No clicks, dropouts or hung notes at the end of the song (the last chord rings out).

## Mixing note

The track faders are a starting point: in a first test the master peaked at +3.5 dB, so they were pulled down about 6 dB (the master should now
stay below 0 dB, but check the meter on your machine). The instruments are not level-matched at the plugin outputs (for example the Midnight
Upright piano is much quieter than the others, which is why this song uses the Rhodes) - balance by ear, or use a plugin's own output-volume bar.
